# Surfaces v9

- The North Star's *Purpose* opens with the end: what the person the project serves is after,
  found by asking "why does that matter?" of each answer until the document has no deeper one.
  What is in the way of it follows — one barrier or several — then *For* and *Not for*. Its new
  trap is a purpose stated as the problem the project's first feature solves, which is only as
  wide as that feature. The *Goal* and *How we know* rows carry the same test. Their trap is
  features written as outcomes and measured by counts of use while the end's own measure is missing.
  *Produce it honestly* asks the owner for the end in their own words and compares it with the
  stated barrier, and the reshape from the older nine-field form asks for the end before it maps
  the *Diagnosis*, which becomes one barrier in its way.
- The ADR conventions carry the note every record writer applies, whichever workflow writes the
  record: where a decision's subject sits outside what the North Star's *Purpose* names, the
  record's context says so in one line.
- A decision record may carry a signpost, its `Revisit when` field: the observable condition that
  would reopen the choice, and where it is read. It is expected on a `Strategy` record and
  editable in place. The North Star's *Strategy choices in force* list shows each title with its
  signpost, under an exact rebuild rule in the North Star conventions, and a reader that finds a
  signpost met brings it to the owner and changes nothing.
- setup-project offers, where the project keeps automated checks, one check that rebuilds the ADR
  index and the strategy list from the records and fails on a difference.
- A project without a North Star gains the `Revisit when` field and the index check.
- Outside the surfaces, the plugin's ticket convention gains the same field for a deferred
  Ticket: a condition in the world the work waits for, read at its source by every picker, with
  a source no picker can read put in front of the person. A store needs no change for it; a
  Ticket whose reopen condition sits only in prose counts as ready, and pickers read that
  condition only once it is moved into the line.
