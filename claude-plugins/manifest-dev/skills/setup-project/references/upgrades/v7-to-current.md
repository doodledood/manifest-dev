# Upgrading a project from v7: what v8 gives it

A project recording v7 keeps its North Star in nine fields, and those fields let chosen options in:
a price, a channel, a launch plan. v8 keeps in the North Star only what decisions are judged
against — what the project is for and whom it serves, how it measures progress, the principles it
holds against evidence, and the bets it rests on. It moves each chosen option to a decision record,
where a later session meets it as a choice it may revisit rather than as the purpose. A value to
reach by a date moves to the work that pursues it. A project with no `NORTH_STAR.md`
gets item 1 only.

Offer each item on its own. Items 1 to 3 go first, because the reshape in item 4 writes records
under the conventions item 1 changes:

1. **The `Strategy` area in the ADR conventions** — the paragraph that follows the *Area*
   paragraph in `../../../figure-out/references/ADR_FORMAT.md`, added at the same place in the
   project's conventions file: a record of a guiding choice — one the project pursues its goal
   through, and that other decisions derive from — carries the area `Strategy`, and the North
   Star's *Strategy choices in force* list holds the titles of those records in effect, so
   writing, superseding or retiring one rebuilds the list in the same act.
2. **The North Star conventions** — where the project keeps a North Star conventions file, the
   spans its v7 copy lacks, offered section by section per *Compare, then offer* in
   `../../SKILL.md`: the opening that says the document holds what decisions are judged against,
   *What a line may be*, *The sections*, *What it anchors*, the provenance note without history,
   the skeleton, and *Produce it honestly*.
3. **The North Star line of the project-surfaces section** — its newest wording from
   `../NORTH_STAR_FORMAT.md` → *The project-surfaces section*, which names what the North Star
   holds and says the choices made along the way live in the decision records. Show it before and
   after.
4. **The North Star reshape** — the project's own `NORTH_STAR.md` moved into the six sections,
   per `RESHAPE_NORTH_STAR.md` beside this file: every line sorted by the line test, each given a
   destination, and nothing written until the owner has ruled on each line. A run with no person
   present to rule flags the reshape in its report and changes nothing in the North Star.
