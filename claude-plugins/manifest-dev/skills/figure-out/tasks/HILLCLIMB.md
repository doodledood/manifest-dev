# HILLCLIMB — probing fuel

Angles that are easy to under-weight when the point is to improve an existing artifact round after round toward a goal — a climb that tries changes, measures them, and keeps one only on a confirmed gain. With several objectives, the **primary** is the one a gain is claimed on and the others are **guards**, which may not get worse beyond a margin. Composes onto `MEASURE.md` and onto the artifact's own file. Considerations, not an agenda. Press the load-bearing ones; priority stays yours.

## Blind-spot probes
- **How careful** — Is this a quick climb — infer the goal, check the measure once, stop at the first flat round — or does something make it thorough: the person asked, a long or unattended run, a wrong result that is costly or hard to undo, objectives that pull against each other?
- **The kind of goal** — Is the goal a number, a behavior of the people served (sign-ups, clicks, retention), how well the artifact serves them, or the requester's own taste? Each takes a different measure.
- **Real use or a prediction** — For a behavior, can real use measure it here: how many people does the artifact reach, and what is the smallest gain a test could detect in how long? Short of that, what can be checked offline — a fresh visitor played as the stated person, guards such as speed — and what may the result then claim beyond a prediction?
- **How the measure could rise while the goal is not served** — What would a version that scores well and serves no one look like? Is each such way guarded, and does a clearly worse version lose on the measure — including a measure the requester brought?
- **What no measure mentions** — What does the artifact do well today that none of the measures covers, and what stops the climb from spending it to buy the measured gain?
- **Where the cheap check and real use differ** — When the full measure is slow, under which conditions people meet the artifact — inputs, settings, devices, motion against a still — could the cheap check see something different, and has it caught a known-worse version under each?
- **What a round can afford** — Does one measurement take seconds or hours, and is one version a small edit or a large change? That decides whether a round tries several versions or one change aimed at a diagnosed cause, and whether it changes the whole artifact or one part with the rest guarded.
- **Past the nearest peak** — How will the climb get past the first version no small change improves: bold attempts, a runner-up as a starting point, a diagnosis of what limits the result?
- **A priority that moves** — If the order of the objectives changes partway, which kept gains were judged only under the old order? A changed measure or priority starts a new climb.
- **A judge that can be fooled** — Where an AI judge is the measure, does a degraded version lose, do identical versions tie, and does the verdict hold when the two versions swap places? What taught it — the requester's reasons as criteria, past results from real use, or only the request?

## Forced trade-offs
- One primary with the rest as guards vs the requester choosing among a few whole versions — which fits when nobody can name the primary?
- Primary gain vs guard loss — how much may each guard lose against the original, not against last round, before a gain on the primary stops being worth it?
- A weighted sum vs floors that veto — are there exchange rates the requester can state over the real ranges, or only lines a version must not cross?
- Many small safe steps vs fewer bold versions — at the traffic this artifact gets, would a small gain even be detectable?
