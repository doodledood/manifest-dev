# UI — probing fuel

Angles that are easy to under-weight before anything a person sees exists — a screen, a deck, a rendered document, a chart. Considerations, not an agenda. Press the load-bearing ones; priority stays yours. Look and feel belong to the design skill's direction.

## Blind-spot probes
- **Every state** — What does each surface show when empty, loading, failed, stale or half-saved, and what survives leaving midway?
- **Who and where** — Which inputs, assistive technology, languages and text direction, and motion settings must it serve, and in which medium — a talk or a reading deck, a PDF, a native app?
- **First look** — On each surface, what does the eye land on first and what path does it follow from there, and is that the order the person's moment needs?
- **Honest figures** — For each number or chart, what are its unit and denominator, how are missing values shown, and is it observed or forecast?

## Forced trade-offs
- Density vs legibility — which does the person's repeated loop need on this surface?
