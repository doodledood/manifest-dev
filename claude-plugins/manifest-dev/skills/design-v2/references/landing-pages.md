# Landing pages

The job is to introduce something, make its value and identity intelligible, and support the next action. Product clarity and expressive art direction can coexist. Select a visual language for this product and audience rather than defaulting to the same neutral SaaS layout every time.

These are curated taste references, not performance rankings. The annotations are the aspects to borrow, not claims about a site's current implementation.

## Approved calibration pair

| Reference | What to borrow |
|---|---|
| [Family](https://family.co/) | Product-native polish and iPhone-like aliveness; let the product's behavior carry personality. |
| [Paper](https://paper.design/) | Visual ambition tied to the tool and what people make with it, rather than unrelated decoration. |

## Established references

| Reference | What to borrow |
|---|---|
| [Linear](https://linear.app/) | Cross-category craft anchor: precise hierarchy, coherent composition and restrained polish. Transfer the discipline, not a mandatory palette or layout. |
| [Attio](https://attio.com/) | Refined product-led presentation; actual information and interactions can provide the visual identity. |
| [Clay](https://www.clay.com/) | Personality and visual storytelling while keeping the product understandable. |
| [Spline](https://spline.design/) | Rich interaction connected to what the product does. |
| [Arc](https://arc.net/) | Polished product communication with color and character. |

## Spectacle boundary

[Active Theory](https://activetheory.net/) is an admired experiential-web reference, not the normal intensity target for a landing page. Borrow its ambition when immersion or emotional impact is part of the job. Preserve a clear explanation and route to the actual product; spectacle that obscures those has crossed the boundary.

## Reserve

[Framer](https://www.framer.com/) and [Resend](https://resend.com/) were liked, but were not the strongest final selections. They can inform a relevant detail without displacing the approved calibration pair or narrowing every new design back to the same software-marketing aesthetic.
