# Representation and information fidelity

Choose how the audience will perceive the relationship before arranging the content: prose, a table, diagram, chart, image, sequence or interaction. A reader should not have to reconstruct a spatial relationship or quantitative pattern from paragraphs when a representation makes it visible. Nor does structural vocabulary by itself require a diagram.

Use complementary representations where they serve different needs: a chart can reveal a pattern while a table supplies exact values. Labels identify, annotations explain, and prose qualifies. Remove needless repeated explanation, not the context or accessible equivalent that makes a representation usable.

## Preserve the information

- Keep referents, notation and categories consistent across marks, labels, controls and neighboring views. Position, size, enclosure, direction and color each convey meaning.
- Check units, denominators, aggregation, dates, missingness and uncertainty. Distinguish observation, calculation, forecast and labeled illustration. A zero is not a missing value; animation and smoothing must not invent evidence.
- Match quantitative scales to the question and encoding. Comparable panels need compatible domains or a clearly explained difference. Disclose consequential truncation and transformations; axes do not all require the same scale or a zero baseline.
- Preserve the important relationship through responsive layout and export. Inspect labels at their delivered size and in supported themes. Critical labels belong in a controllable text layer, not an unverified generated image.
- Provide equivalent access to the information the task needs, including relationships, uncertainty and relevant interaction states. A one-sentence takeaway or a raw data table may be insufficient. Hide a graphic from assistive technology only when its needed meaning is available elsewhere.

Inspect the actual comparison or explanation. A figure count, a familiar chart type or valid SVG does not establish that the audience can interpret it correctly.
