# Collections

Help people find something worth opening, whether they know its name or are browsing.

Make items recognizable through meaningful titles, concise descriptions, and previews that show what is inside. Choose rows, cards, or another arrangement according to what people compare. Use grouping, search, and filters when they reduce the effort of finding a relevant item.

Give featured work prominence while keeping other destinations easy to reach. Preserve orientation as the collection changes. A preview can invite exploration while the route to the item remains direct; keep essential choices available without requiring discovery of a hidden interaction.
