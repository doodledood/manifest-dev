# Graphics

Make the central message readable at a glance and understandable when the piece is encountered on its own.

Build a strong hierarchy around the relationship, announcement, or claim that matters. Choose composition, scale, contrast, and economical labeling to make that meaning visible. Give supporting context a clear secondary role.

Compose for the actual viewing size and distance. Keep essential information within the part that survives cropping or sharing, and include enough context to interpret a detached figure. Let decorative detail reinforce the subject while the message remains apparent.
