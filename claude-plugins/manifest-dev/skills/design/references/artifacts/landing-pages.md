# Landing pages

Make the offering's promise and the visitor's next action clear, with enough substance to decide whether it is for them.

Compose the first screen around the reason to care and something concrete that makes it believable. Default to one headline, with any explanation of a featured visual on or beside it. Size the composition by viewport height as well as width.

Let the visitor's arrival context determine how much persuasion they need. Develop the page through relevant demonstrations, evidence, and answers to meaningful objections. Keep copy and controls simple, and put practical terms near the action they qualify. A visitor ready to act can do so without completing the story.
