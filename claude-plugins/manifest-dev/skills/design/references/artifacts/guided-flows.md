# Guided flows

Make a task with dependent choices feel understandable and manageable from entry to completion.

Sequence decisions according to what the person knows and what later steps need. Group related inputs, explain consequential choices where they occur, and ask for information when its purpose is apparent. Show enough progress and context for the person to know what remains.

Preserve useful work when someone goes back or corrects a mistake. Place errors beside the affected input with a clear path to correction. Make consequential commitments reviewable before submission, and give completion a distinct state that explains the result and the next useful action.
