# Reference

Make a specific answer easy to locate, interpret, compare, and return to.

Organize around the questions people bring and the terms they recognize. Use predictable headings, precise labels, and consistent treatment of comparable values. Keep units, definitions, sources, and uncertainty close to the claims they qualify.

Put the answer and the controls that determine it within easy reach. Let supporting explanations unfold without displacing the information being inspected. Make individual sections directly reachable where the medium permits it. Give dense material a clear hierarchy so the person can retrieve one fact without reading everything.
