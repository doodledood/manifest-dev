# Demonstrative

Make the value tangible: the person understands what happens and wants to try it.

Make a meaningful transformation the centerpiece. Frame a recognizable input, the consequential action, and a legible result. Use a close view, selective detail, strong visual contrast between states, and purposeful transitions to make cause and effect satisfying.

Keep explanations beside the change they explain. Give people time to inspect the result, and keep the path into the real experience apparent. In static media, compose the key states so their relationship reads at a glance. Show enough of the actual behavior to make the demonstration credible.
