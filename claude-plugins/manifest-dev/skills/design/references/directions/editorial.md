# Editorial

Make the subject feel worth sustained attention, with the character and considered composition of excellent editorial design.

Build around a sharp idea and a clear hierarchy of reading. Use expressive typography, deliberate spacing, distinctive imagery, captions, and evidence that rewards closer attention. Vary scale and density to create rhythm; let a major idea occupy space and supporting detail stay close to its claim.

Give each section a reason to exist and each visual a relationship to the argument. Make routes into the material apparent so a person can browse, follow the sequence, or return to something specific.
