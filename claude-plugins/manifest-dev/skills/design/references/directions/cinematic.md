# Cinematic

Make the person feel wowed without feeling overwhelmed, while keeping the meaning and next action clear.

Make it cinematic and immersive: one striking animated centerpiece, dimensional materials, dramatic lighting, generous negative space, bold typography, and restrained color.

Draw the visual idea from what the subject does or makes possible. Concentrate the spectacle there; keep the surrounding copy and controls simple. Put any explanation of the centerpiece on or beside it. Choreograph motion around a meaningful reveal, with enough stillness to take it in. For static media, express the same depth and drama through composition and lighting.
