# Human

Create recognition, warmth, and grounded trust: the person feels understood and can imagine taking the next step.

Begin with a recognizable situation and the change that matters to the people in it. Use specific, natural language, authentic details, and real people, places, or work where available. Choose approachable typography, comfortable spacing, and imagery with a sense of presence.

Make the experience and the people behind it understandable. Put practical reassurance beside the choice that raises the concern, supported by something the person can inspect. Let warmth come from relevance and care; preserve the seriousness of difficult situations.
