# Campaign

Make one proposition memorable and create momentum toward a clear action.

Commit to a strong visual concept and a distinctive voice. Use assertive typography, decisive color, striking imagery, and deliberate repetition to make the message stick. Compose for recognition at a glance, then give the person concrete reasons to care.

Develop the proposition through a short persuasive progression suited to the medium. Let the action complete that progression. Match the energy to the cause, event, or offer, and ground urgency in real circumstances. Keep the central idea recognizable across sections or related pieces.
