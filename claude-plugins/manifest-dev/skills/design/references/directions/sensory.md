# Sensory

Make the offering feel tangible and desirable, so a person can imagine experiencing it.

Let texture, material, light, scale, and setting carry the visual story. Pair close detail with wider context: the surface of an object and how it sits in a hand, the food and the table. Give imagery room to breathe, with typography and color drawn from the subject.

Use motion, where the medium permits it, to reveal qualities such as weight, softness, freshness, or craftsmanship. Let practical information remain easy to find at the moment desire turns into a decision.
