# Dated calibration — currently-overused looks

**As of 2026-09.** This file rots in months and is refreshed by observation, not extended: every list of banned looks eventually mints the next cliché, because whatever replaces the banned default becomes the new default. So treat these as convergence attractors to avoid *where the choice is free* — a brief that asks for one gets it — and prefer the durable counter over growing this list: derive palette, faces, and signature moves from the subject's own world (`SKILL.md` Decision 3; `craft.md` style derivation).

## Cliché clusters, by generation

- **First-order (pre-2026):** Inter or Roboto picked by reflex; indigo-to-purple gradient hero on white; three rounded feature cards; centered everything; emoji as icons; border *and* shadow on every card; frosted-glass panels by default; sparkle and rocket emoji in headings.
- **Second-order (the corrected look, now itself the cliché):** warm cream background (#F4F1EA class) + high-contrast serif + terracotta accent; near-black page with one acid-green or vermilion accent; broadsheet hairlines over dense columns; Space Grotesk as the "safe distinctive" face; an accent bar or rail on rounded cards.
- **Illustration:** flat, bendy-limbed corporate figures — the highest-probability "add illustrations" output; reads as templated everywhere.
- **Gradients:** the aurora/mesh wave has peaked. What reads crafted now: near-monochrome subtle gradients, grain-textured, or mesh as deliberate brand art. Durable form: a gradient earns its place when its hues come from the subject's palette and text over it still passes contrast.

## How to use this file

1. When writing the token block, list the 3–5 clusters above nearest to the artifact's genre as an explicit Don'ts section, each with the alternative ("not Inter-by-reflex — derive the face from the subject's era and material").
2. A face or look on this list chosen *by derivation* is legitimate — the defect is the reflex, not the artifact. Say what derived it.
3. When the as-of date is more than ~6 months old, distrust the specifics and keep only the mechanism: whatever look now dominates freshly generated pages is the thing to counter-derive against.
