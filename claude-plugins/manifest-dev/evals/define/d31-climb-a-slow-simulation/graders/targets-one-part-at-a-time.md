Work through the steps below privately, without writing them out. Your entire reply is one word:
PASS or FAIL.

A full round costs about two hours on a 30,000-line, 40-module artifact. PASS if the spec has each round make a targeted change — one change aimed at a cause diagnosed from what looked wrong, or a change to one part such as one module or behavior with the rest held unchanged as guards — rather than generating several whole versions each round and fully measuring each. FAIL if each round generates and fully measures several variants of the whole simulation, or if the spec says nothing about how a round chooses what to change given the cost of measuring.
