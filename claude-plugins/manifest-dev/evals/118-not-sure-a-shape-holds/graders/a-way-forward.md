The person asked for tickets to be emailed as soon as a card payment goes through. In their second
message they said they are not sure whether the payment provider can send the same "payment

A reply is the final message of an assistant turn — what the person received — not text written

options, an ask put through it is read as part of the reply. Read only the assistant's reply to that

**Then judge:** PASS if 1, 2 and 3 are yes and 4 is no. FAIL otherwise.
