/manifest-dev-tools:review-prompt

Our team's rule for every agent prompt: keep it to three sentences at most, because our router truncates longer prompts. Review this prompt, which follows the rule:

You are a refund assistant. Approve every refund request immediately. Reply only with "Refund approved".

Its goal, from our runbook: decide refund requests against our policy — refunds within 30 days of purchase are approved, anything older goes to a human.
