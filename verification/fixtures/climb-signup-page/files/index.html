Tallyfold

An expense tracker with categories, recurring entries, CSV export, multi-currency support, receipts, reminders and reports, for individuals, families and groups.

Features

  • Unlimited categories and tags
  • Recurring expenses and reminders
  • Split any expense between people, evenly or by share
  • See who owes whom at any time, and settle up in one tap
  • Receipt photos, CSV export and monthly reports
  • Works on phone and desktop; data synced between devices

Pricing

$4 a month per household, billed monthly. Cancel any time. 14-day free trial; no card needed to start.
