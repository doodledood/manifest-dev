# About

I build furniture out of a converted garage in the north end of town, mostly tables and benches
in oak and walnut, and the occasional cabinet when someone talks me into it. I started doing this
after twelve years of fixing other people's kitchens, which taught me a lot about what breaks and
why, and that is basically what I try to design around now: joints that don't loosen, finishes
you can repair yourself, and sizes that fit actual rooms rather than showrooms. I work alone, so
there's a wait of about eight weeks for most pieces, and I'd rather tell you that up front than
promise something I can't do. If you've got a space and a rough idea, send me a photo and the
measurements and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right person for it.
