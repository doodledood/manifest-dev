# Decisions

Recommendation results have five visible slots on the first shelf. The interface has no
second page. Sessions from the last research sample: 72 of 100 participants picked from
that first shelf without searching again. This is a small sample, not a population estimate.

The paid-placement proposal is requested by the partnerships lead: fill three slots with
sponsored books, independent of match score, then use the remaining slots for relevance.
The product steward owns the trade-off between discovery and sponsor income. That steward
has not yet assessed this proposal. The sponsor pilot can be cancelled before rollout.
