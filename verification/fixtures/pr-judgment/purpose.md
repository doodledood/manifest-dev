# Product direction

Shelf helps community reading groups find books that fit a member's stated interests.
Its primary goal is useful discovery on the first shelf, especially for members who
browse only once a month. Donations cover operations; a sustainable sponsor channel
is a secondary goal. These are product goals to weigh, not a prohibition on promotion.
The product steward decides commercial trade-offs after reviewing the user effect.
A product request sets the requested implementation, not the final release decision.
