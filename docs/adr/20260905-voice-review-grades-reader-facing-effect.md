# ADR: Voice review grades reader-facing effect

## Status
Accepted

## Area
Writing review

## Context

The human-voice reference graded AI identifiability, called MEDIUM informational despite a blocking threshold, and carried detector statistics and arbitrary shape rules. Those rules do not establish whether a text serves its reader or preserves its author's voice.

## Decision

Retain review-writing's no-MEDIUM-or-higher threshold. Grade the consequence for meaning, audience, intended voice, and craft. Remove detector statistics and arbitrary shape prescriptions; punctuation, grammatical sentences, and repeated structures are not defects on their own. Preserve author-specific guidance and grounded experience.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep detector-based grading: makes severity depend on an unsupported authorship inference.
- Make every voice observation advisory: weakens the existing acceptance threshold rather than correcting its conflicting reference.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Findings identify concrete consequences and the severity reference agrees with the owning skill.

### Negative

- Voice and craft still require judgment against the brief and supplied writing.

## Source

The review-writing skill owns the blocking threshold. Its shared prose floor and documentation and voice references supply the evaluation standards.
