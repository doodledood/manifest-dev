Read the findings after trying the pages

Blind reviewers preferred the first original-skill sample in all three briefs. They also preferred it to the second original-skill generation. This small pilot does not establish that the rewrite improves design—or that the differences are caused by the instructions.

  • Landing: the original was more compact; the final explains acknowledgement more explicitly but adds reading and framing.
  • Analytics: the original gives comparison and detail the strongest shared view; the final has a cohesive ledger treatment and a good phone detail jump.
  • Presentation: the original makes the evidence gap visible; the final has a strong opening but a heavier finish.

Known defects remain in the raw samples: the intermediate landing has contradictory receipt icons; the repeat-original analytics can leave a keyboard-selected route offscreen. The final presentation hides direct slide jumps on phones. None was silently repaired.
