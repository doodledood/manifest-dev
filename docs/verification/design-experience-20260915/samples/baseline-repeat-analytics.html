How to read this view

One consistent denominator

On time means arriving no more than five minutes after the scheduled arrival. The rate is on-time trips ÷ all scheduled trips, including cancellations.

Like-for-like periods

The latest four weeks are compared with the preceding four weeks within the same day type. A change from 92% to 87% is −5 percentage points, not −5%.

A signal, not a diagnosis

Cancellations are separate counts, not additional late arrivals to add to a percentage. These counts do not establish a cause for delays.
