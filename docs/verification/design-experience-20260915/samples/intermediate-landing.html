The idea behind Relay

The essentials travel
with the work.

  1. 01 /

    Keep the why.

    Put settled decisions and still-open questions on the same page. A handoff doesn’t have to pretend everything is resolved.

  2. 02 /

    Name who’s next.

    Keep the next owner beside the next step. “Over to you” comes with a clear place to start.

  3. 03 /

    Know it landed.

    The recipient acknowledges the handoff. The sender can see it was received—not that the work is already done.
