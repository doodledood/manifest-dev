Reading these numbers

On time means arrival no more than five minutes after schedule. The rate is on-time trips ÷ all scheduled trips, including cancellations. “pp” means percentage points.

These counts identify patterns, not causes. Late arrivals are scheduled trips minus on-time trips and cancellations. Each route is compared with its own preceding four weeks in the same day type.
