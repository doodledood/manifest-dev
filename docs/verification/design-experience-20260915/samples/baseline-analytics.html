What “on time” means

Arrival no more than five minutes after the scheduled arrival. On-time rate = on-time trips ÷ all scheduled trips, including cancellations. Changes compare with the same day type in the preceding four weeks.

A comparison, not a diagnosis

These fictional demonstration figures cover the latest four weeks and the preceding four weeks. They identify differences worth investigating; they do not establish what caused a delay.
