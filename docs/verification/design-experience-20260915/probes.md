# Comparison probes

Frozen before inspecting the final-package and repeat-baseline outputs, after the first baseline and new-core/old-reference outputs existed. These are brief-derived checks and review prompts, not instructions sent to the generators. They do not constitute a powered or held-out benchmark.

## Shared observations

Inspect the actual pages at 1440×900 and 390×844, plus 320 CSS px reflow. Use keyboard and pointer controls; inspect visible focus and selected states. Exercise ordinary motion and the reduced-motion alternative, including repeated actions. Report console errors, broken controls, inaccessible content and unsupported checks. A static image cannot establish interaction quality; source syntax cannot establish access.

Judge what becomes understandable and what remains hard to find, compare or remember. Identify the organizing visual idea, whether it fits the subject and task, and whether it survives active use. Consider hierarchy, relationships, type, imagery, density and motion together. Quiet and expressive treatments are both legitimate; a coherent conventional layout can beat a novel one.

Record gains and losses per artifact, not a single aggregate aesthetic score. A finding needs a rendered or behavioral consequence. Literal spacing counts, a particular font count, presence of animation, a CSS media query or an assertion-style headline are observations—not automatic quality scores. Check whether any changed implementation habit actually harms consistency, understanding or use.

Use the same brief-derived criteria for every arm. The reviewer may consult both original and revised design standards, but must distinguish an applicable access/functional failure from compliance with a retired stylistic prescription. A rule added or removed by the candidate cannot by itself make the candidate win.

## Relay landing page

- Can the viewer identify the handoff product, its intended studio audience and the next action?
- Can they find the fictional $12/workspace/month price, 14-day trial and no-credit-card term without mistaking the concept for a live signup?
- Does **Try a handoff** reach the sample project? Are its decision, open question, next owner and Thursday print-proof step preserved?
- Acknowledge and undo/reset twice. Does the displayed state agree with the action? Is it clear that only the local example changes? Inspect feedback and focus, not just whether the button label changes.
- Do the product explanation, example and pricing feel like one composition? Does repetition supply needed context or compete with it?

## Interpretation boundary

The original skill gets a second independent generation per brief to expose some within-arm variation. The intermediate uses the new core with the old references; the final uses revised references plus two core clarifications and updated loading pointers. Their difference is therefore not a pure reference-deletion ablation. No participant study, random-seed pairing or cross-model replication is present. Findings describe these artifacts; they cannot establish audience recall, conversion, general skill efficacy or a causal effect of a particular prompt line.
